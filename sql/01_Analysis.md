# SQL Analysis Summary

This document summarizes the key findings from the SQL analysis performed on the Retail Superstore dataset.

---

## 🔹 Global Performance

- The company generated strong revenue growth after 2015.
- 2016 recorded the highest Year-over-Year sales growth (+29.47%).
- Profit generally follows sales growth, although growth slowed slightly in 2017.

---

## 🔹 Seasonality

- Strong sales peaks observed in November and December.
- Lower sales performance between March and July.
- Profit trend generally follows sales seasonality.

---

## 🔹 Category Insights

- Some sub-categories generate high sales but low profitability.
- Certain product segments operate at negative margins.

---

## 🔹 Geographic Insights

- Profitability varies significantly by region.
- Some cities show high sales but negative profit, indicating margin pressure.

---

## 🔹 Strategic Observations

- 2015 shows an interesting divergence: sales declined slightly while profit increased.
- 2017 shows continued growth but with slower profit expansion, suggesting potential margin compression.

---

This analysis serves as the foundation for the Power BI dashboard development.
