# KPI Definitions

## Total Sales
SUM(Sales)

## Total Profit
SUM(Profit)

## Profit Margin (%)
(SUM(Profit) / SUM(Sales)) * 100

## Total Orders
COUNT(DISTINCT Order ID)

## Average Order Value (AOV)
Total Sales / Total Orders

## Year-over-Year Growth (YoY %)
(Current Year - Previous Year) / Previous Year * 100
